# Official ProKeeper Scorebook Guide

ProKeeper provides an authentic, high-fidelity digital reproduction of the official regulation basketball scorebook.

---

## 1. Running Score Matrix

The running score section tracks points 1 through 140+ in chronological sequence:
* **Point Number**: The cumulative team score index.
* **Jersey Number**: Scribed inside or adjacent to the point box indicating who scored the point.
* **Diagonal Slash**: Visual mark crossing the point tally when made.
* **Period Demarcations**: Color changes and period separators denote scoring rhythm across quarters and overtime.

---

## 2. Individual Foul Matrix

Every roster athlete is tracked with:
* **Personal Fouls 1 through 5**: Marked with an `X` as fouls occur. Reaching 5 triggers the Disqualification (DQ) warning.
* **Technical Fouls (TF)**: Numbered separately for bench and administrative records.
* **Field Goal Breakdown**: Detailed 2-point makes, 3-point makes, and free throws made/attempted ($FTM - FTA$).

---

## 3. PDF & Print Export

The scorebook layout is optimized with dedicated print stylesheets:
* Access via `/scorebook/{game_code}`.
* Click **Print / Save PDF** to generate an official 8.5x11 landscape scorebook document ready for referee signature and archival.
* Download a server-rendered PDF via `/export/scorebook/{game_code}.pdf`.
