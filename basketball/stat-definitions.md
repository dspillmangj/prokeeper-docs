# Basketball Stat Definitions & Scoring Rules

ProKeeper strictly follows NCAA and NFHS statistical manual guidelines for calculating official box scores and player metrics.

---

## Shooting & Scoring Rules

* **Field Goal Attempt (FGA)**: Any live ball attempt toward the basket, whether made or missed, unless blocked/fouled during the shooting motion before the release without a make.
* **3-Point Field Goal Attempt (3PA)**: Any field goal shot released from behind the collegiate/high school 3-point line. A 3-point attempt also increments total FGA by 1.
* **Free Throw Attempt (FTA)**: An unhindered shot from the free-throw line awarded after a foul. Technical fouls and flagrant fouls are included.
* **Field Goal Percentage (FG%)**:
  $$\text{FG\%} = \frac{\text{FGM}}{\text{FGA}}$$
* **Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%)**:
  $$\text{eFG\%} = \frac{\text{FGM} + 0.5 \times \text{3PM}}{\text{FGA}}$$

---

## Rebounds

* **Defensive Rebound (DREB)**: Credited to an individual defensive player who retrieves a missed field goal or missed final free throw attempt.
* **Offensive Rebound (OREB)**: Credited to an offensive player who retrieves their team's missed shot, keeping possession alive.

---

## Playmaking & Defense

* **Assist (AST)**: A pass directly leading to a made basket by a teammate where the recipient scores with immediate rhythm.
* **Steal (STL)**: Credited to a defensive player who actively takes the ball away from an offensive player or intercepts a pass.
* **Block (BLK)**: Credited when a defender deflects a shot attempt, preventing it from reaching the rim.
* **Swat / Deflection (SWAT)**: Defensive touch deflecting a pass or loose ball out of bounds or forcing a reset.

---

## Turnovers

* **Passing Turnover (P)**: Errant pass intercepted or thrown out of bounds.
* **Fumble Turnover (U)**: Ball lost during dribbling, gathering, or mishandling.
* **Violation Turnover (I)**: Traveling, 3 seconds in the key, 5-second closely guarded, backcourt violation, or shot clock violation.

---

## Fouls & Disqualification

* **Personal Foul (PF)**: Illegal contact against an opponent. 5 personal fouls result in player disqualification (DQ).
* **Offensive Foul (OF)**: Player-control foul or illegal screen. Does not award free throws to the opponent but turns over possession.
* **Technical Foul (TF)**: Unsportsmanlike conduct by a player or bench personnel.
