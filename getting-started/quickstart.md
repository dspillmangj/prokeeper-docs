# Quickstart Guide

Get up and running with ProKeeper in under 5 minutes. This guide walks you through setting up an organization account, creating your team roster, and launching your first live stat-tracking session.

---

## 1. Create an Organization Account

1. Navigate to `https://app.prokeeper.com/register`.
2. Enter your **School or Organization Name** (e.g., *Lighthouse Christian Academy*).
3. Enter your administrator name, email address, and secure password.
4. Click **Register Organization** to automatically provision your athletic portal and enter the Command Center.

---

## 2. Set Up a Team & Roster

1. From the top navigation bar, select **Teams & Rosters**.
2. Click **Create New Team**.
3. Choose the sport (**Basketball** or **Volleyball**), gender, competition level (Varsity, JV, Freshman), and active season.
4. Once the team profile is created, use the **Add Player** panel to add players with their jersey numbers and default positions.
5. Check the **Starter** checkbox for your initial starting lineup (5 starters for basketball, 6 for volleyball).

---

## 3. Launch a Live Game Session

1. In the top navigation, click **New Game**.
2. Select your sport and choose your home team roster.
3. Type the visiting team name (or select an existing away roster).
4. Click **Launch Game Operator** to open the real-time scoring interface.

---

## 4. Track Live Stats via Keyboard

Once the operator HUD loads:
* Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to start or stop the game clock.
* To enter a stat, type the command into the rapid entry bar and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>:
  * `23-X` -> Home #23 made a 2-point field goal (+2 pts).
  * `11=M` -> Away #11 made a 3-point field goal (+3 pts).
  * `33-D` -> Home #33 grabbed a defensive rebound.
  * `05-F` -> Home #05 committed a personal foul.
* To undo a mistake, press the **Undo** button or use the undo shortcut.

---

## 5. Share with Fans & Scorers

Every game generates a unique 6-character access code (e.g. `EAG101`):
* **Live Scoreboard**: `https://app.prokeeper.com/live/EAG101`
* **Official Scorebook**: `https://app.prokeeper.com/scorebook/EAG101`
* **Official PDF Export**: `https://app.prokeeper.com/export/scorebook/EAG101.pdf`
