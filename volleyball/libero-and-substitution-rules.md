# Libero & Substitution Rules

Handling specialized defensive specialists and substitution limits under NCAA / NFHS volleyball regulations.

---

## Libero Rules & Replacements

1. **Contrasting Uniform**: The Libero must wear a jersey that distinctly contrasts in color with the rest of the team.
2. **Back-Row Only**: The Libero is restricted to back-row play (Positions 1, 6, and 5) and cannot attack the ball above net height.
3. **Unlimited Replacements**: Libero replacements do not count toward the team's total substitution limit per set.
4. **Serving**: Under NCAA/NFHS rules, the Libero may serve in one designated rotation position per set.

---

## Substitution Limits

* Standard High School (NFHS): Up to 18 substitutions per set.
* Standard College (NCAA): Up to 15 substitutions per set.
* Player Pairing: Once player A replaces player B in a rotation, only player B can substitute back in for player A during that set.
